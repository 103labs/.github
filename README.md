# 103 Labs

**103 Labs** is an independent research initiative at the intersection of data engineering, applied artificial intelligence, and spatial modeling. We are dedicated to conducting independent research and applying AI to extract insights from complex datasets and address scientific and industrial challenges.

---

### Focus Areas

*   **Spatial & Environmental Data Modeling**: Geospatial analysis, environmental integration, and predictive spatial systems.
*   **Applied Artificial Intelligence**: Leveraging machine learning workflows for complex, real-world domain problems.
*   **Data Engineering**: Building robust, scalable pipelines and architectures to handle complex, large-scale datasets.
*   **Software Engineering**: Research insights on software engineering topics such as agile software development.
